# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** {{LEGAL_VERSION}}

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. This page says exactly what AI Hiring System uses.

## What we use

| Name | Type | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_streamlit_xsrf` | Cookie, **strictly necessary** | A security token from the Streamlit framework that stops other websites from submitting forms on your behalf (CSRF protection) | Until you close the browser (session) |
| `stSidebarSectionsState-…` | Browser local storage, strictly necessary | Remembers whether the framework's side menu is open | Until you clear site data |

Your sign-in is kept in the page's memory only, so closing the tab signs you out. We do not store your password or login token in a cookie.

## What we do not use
No analytics, no advertising, no social-media or tracking cookies, and no tools that follow you across other websites. Because we only use strictly necessary storage, there are no optional cookies to switch on or off.

## Things outside our control
- **Hosting.** The website is delivered through Streamlit Community Cloud and the API through Render. These providers may set their own cookies or collect technical data (for example to keep their service running or to measure traffic). We do not control that; see their policies.
- **Fonts.** Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google directly, which shows Google your IP address, but we do not add any cookies for it.

## Your choices
You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the security cookie, forms on the site may stop working.

## Changes
If we ever add anything beyond strictly necessary storage (for example analytics), we will update this page and ask for your permission **before** using it.
