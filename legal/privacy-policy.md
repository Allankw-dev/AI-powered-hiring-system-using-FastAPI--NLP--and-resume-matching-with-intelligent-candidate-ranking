## 3. Automated scoring and human decisions

When you apply for a job, software compares your resume with the job post and produces scores: text similarity (45%), skills match (25%), experience (20%) and a resume-consistency check (10%), combined into an overall score and a rank among that job's applicants. The consistency check looks for patterns such as keyword stuffing or inconsistent dates; it can be wrong, a flag is not proof of anything, and it is only a prompt for a human to look closer.

**The software never accepts or rejects anyone.** A recruiter reviews the ranked list and makes every decision, and may ignore the scores. You can see your own scores and matched and missing skills in your dashboard. If you would like a person to re-review your application, or an explanation of a score, contact us at the address above.

## 4. Who can see your data

- **Recruiters (administrators)** of this platform can see your name, email, resumes, applications and scores for the jobs you apply to. Other candidates cannot see your data.
- **Service providers (processors)** that run the service for us:

| Provider | What it does | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Render | Hosts the API server | Everything the API processes, plus server logs |
| Streamlit Community Cloud | Hosts the website you see | Page requests; what you type into forms passes through it to the API |
| Supabase | Hosts the database | All stored data listed above |
| Resend (or Gmail SMTP) | Sends emails | Recipient address and the email text |
| Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini) | Power the chat assistant, only when you use it | The chat messages you type. Do not type personal data into the chat. |
| Google Fonts | Delivers the typefaces | Your browser contacts Google directly, which reveals your IP address to Google |

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

**International transfers.** These providers may process data outside Kenya (for example in the United States or the EU). We use providers that publish their own data-protection commitments, but we cannot control their infrastructure.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Your account, resumes, applications, consent records and the emails recruiters sent you: **until you delete your account** (you can do it yourself at any time, see section 6).
- Sign-in codes and reset links: minutes to one hour, then they stop working.
- When you delete your account the data is erased from our live database immediately. Copies held in our database provider's backups are overwritten on the provider's normal backup cycle.
- Technical logs: kept by our hosting providers under their own retention settings, normally a short period.
- If you unsubscribe from application emails, we keep your address on a suppression list only so that we do not email you again; deleting your account removes it.

## 7. Security

We protect data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), one-way password hashing, mandatory two-step sign-in for administrators, access controls so candidates only reach their own data, rate limiting and activity logging. No online service is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
